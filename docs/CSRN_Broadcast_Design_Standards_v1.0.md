# CSRN Broadcast Design Standards v1.0

## Identity
All reusable graphics must remain recognizable as CSRN assets without relying on team colors or text.

## Palette
- Scarlet: `#B5121B`
- Broadcast Black: `#111111`
- Graphite: `#232323`
- Silver: `#BFC5CC`
- White: `#F5F5F5`

## Metallic Typography
Event titles, position labels, and jersey numbers use restrained brushed-silver or scarlet-metal gradients, a one-pixel upper highlight, and a dark lower shadow. Avoid chrome, broad glows, and visible white outlines.

## Geometry
Use consistent rounded corners, thin light borders, subtle shadows, and safe padding. Possession icons and similar indicators may intentionally extend beyond the base frame.

## Sponsor Presentation
Paid sponsor names must be readable at mobile viewing sizes. Sponsor bars are hidden when no sponsor is assigned. Logos preserve aspect ratio and are sourced through Asset Manager.

## Animation
Default entrance/exit animations are short slide-and-fade transitions compatible with OBS Browser Sources.

## Accessibility
Maintain strong contrast, avoid fuzzy strokes, and test at the expected 45% OBS preview scale and on mobile.
