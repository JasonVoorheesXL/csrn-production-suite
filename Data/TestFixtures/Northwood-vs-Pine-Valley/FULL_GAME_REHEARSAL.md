# CSRN Fictional Full-Game Rehearsal

All schools, players, sponsors, logos, portraits, and events in this fixture are fictional.

## Create the game

In Game Manager, select **New Broadcast** and create a Football broadcast using:

- Home: Northwood High School / Northwood Knights
- Visitor: Pine Valley High School / Pine Valley Panthers
- Season: 2026
- Level: Varsity
- Venue: Knight Stadium (should auto-populate from Northwood High School)
- Game type: Regular Season

Assign Riverbend Credit Union during live-game sponsor testing.
Assign Ace Automotive to the postgame article or Player of the Game activity.
