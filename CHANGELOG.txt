LIMITATIONS
- This is an NFHS-oriented enforcement foundation, not yet a complete rulebook implementation.
- Half-the-distance, spot fouls, multiple live-ball fouls, and complex enforcement require statistician quick correction.
- Event editing recalculates event-derived statistics and can correct current state; full downstream drive reconstruction remains planned.
